#!/usr/bin/env python
"""Trial A2: does deleting Il1r1 move the Wnt programme in Kras-mutant AT2-derived clones?

ROUTE A AS SPECIFIED IS IMPOSSIBLE, AND THIS TRIAL EXISTS BECAUSE OF THAT. The
assessment in this folder proposed re-asking England et al. 2025's Figure S2L to
S2N, which reports Axin2 upregulated in the Il1r1-lineage ZsGreen-positive
fraction and is the ONLY direct measurement of Axin2 and Il1r1 overlap in the
primary literature. It said the data were already local in GSE247505. That was
wrong, and checked rather than assumed: the superseries and both its subseries
contain zero mentions of ZsGreen, and the NCBI link service returns exactly
three GEO series for that paper, none of which holds the sorted ZsGreen
libraries. **The field's only direct evidence for the overlap cannot be
re-examined, because it was never deposited.** Row C148 records that.

WHAT THE DEPOSIT DOES HOLD IS A BETTER QUESTION. GSE247504 carries
Sftpc-CreERT2; Red2Kras clones on an Il1r1 heterozygous against an Il1r1
homozygous-deleted background, at two weeks and twelve weeks, two libraries per
group. That is a genetic perturbation of IL-1 receptor dosage with a Wnt readout
available in the same cells, and it tests the direction Choi et al. 2020
hypothesised in the very Discussion sentence that opened this branch:

    "suggesting that crosstalk between IL-1beta and Wnt signaling underlies
     control of cell fate transitions from naive AT2 to primed cell states"

The literature scan for this folder established that IL-1 to Wnt in AT2 cells is
NOT established anywhere: Choi proposed it and did not test it, and no
subsequent paper has. The reverse direction IS established and almost never
cited: Aumiller et al. 2013 found canonical Wnt induces IL-1beta in primary AT2
cells. So this trial asks the untested half of a crosstalk whose other half is
known, which is a better use of the deposit than re-deriving a correlation.

THE CEILING, STATED FIRST BECAUSE IT GOVERNS EVERYTHING. Two libraries per
group. Under a permutation of four labels into two groups of two there are six
orderings, so **the smallest attainable p is 1 over 6, about 0.167, and NOTHING
this trial produces can be significant at any conventional threshold.** The
trial therefore does not compute a p value. It reports COMPLETE SEPARATION, that
both homozygous libraries fall outside both heterozygous libraries, or the
absence of it, and it is registered Descriptive only whatever it finds. Mice per
library are not reported in the GEO metadata, so the library is the unit and the
mouse-level n is unknown and is not assumed.

FROZEN RULES.

R1 UNIT AND SCOPE. The library. Ten Expt2 libraries are read; the contrast is
   Il1r1 heterozygous against homozygous, separately at two weeks and at twelve
   weeks, two libraries per group. The Confetti libraries are non-Kras controls
   and are reported beside them without entering any contrast.

R2 LABEL CORROBORATION, AND IT RUNS BEFORE ANY WNT QUANTITY IS COMPUTED. The
   deposited genotype labels are checked on Il1r1 itself: the homozygous
   libraries must carry less Il1r1 than the heterozygous ones at the same
   timepoint. If they do not, the labels are not usable, or the conditional
   allele leaves the three-prime end intact and three-prime counting cannot see
   the deletion. Either way the trial refuses, because a genotype contrast whose
   genotype cannot be verified is not a contrast. This is rule R2 of the
   neighbouring branch, which caught an inverted suffix map in another deposit.

R3 READOUT. A Wnt target module, pseudobulk per library: summed counts of Axin2,
   Nkd1, Tcf7l2, Lef1, Notum and Wif1 over summed total counts, per ten
   thousand. Pseudobulk rather than per cell because the unit is the library,
   and a per-cell statistic here would be pseudoreplication of the kind this
   repository refuses.

R4 THE NULL, over gene sets rather than over cells, which is the lesson trial M3
   paid for. 300 random six-gene modules matched to the Wnt module on each
   gene's total expression within a factor of two, excluding every gene named in
   this trial. The observed difference between groups is read against the
   distribution of their differences.

R5 THE READING, fixed before it was seen.
   (a) complete separation AND the Wnt module's difference outside the central
       95 per cent of the matched-module null: the Wnt programme moves with
       Il1r1 dosage, Descriptive only.
   (b) complete separation without clearing the null: the separation is within
       what random modules do at this sample size; reported as not
       distinguishable.
   (c) no complete separation: no movement detectable at this sample size.
   (d) R2 failing: not computable.

SECTION U.
 U1 Fewer than 200 cells in any library entering a contrast: not computable.
 U2 Il1r1 not lower in both homozygous libraries than in both heterozygous ones
    at a timepoint: R2 fails and that timepoint is not computable.
 U3 Fewer than 200 usable matched modules: not computable.
 U4 The trial reports no p value and no significance claim, at any outcome.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import sys
from pathlib import Path

sys.stdout.reconfigure(encoding="utf-8")

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE.parents[1] / "datp_epigenetics" / "trials"))
from multiome_utils import REPO, RunRecord, df_to_markdown  # noqa: E402
sys.path.insert(0, str(REPO / "Thesis" / "gate1_04_sikkema_2023_hlca" / "trials"))
from trial_utils import read_mtx_triplet  # noqa: E402

OUT = HERE / "a2_does_il1r1_deletion_move_wnt"
OUT.mkdir(exist_ok=True)
RAW = REPO / "raw_data" / "GSE247505" / "GSE247505_RAW"

WNT = ["Axin2", "Nkd1", "Tcf7l2", "Lef1", "Notum", "Wif1"]
NAMED = set(WNT + ["Il1r1", "Il1rap", "Sftpc", "Krt8", "Cldn4", "Kras", "Myd88", "Nfkbia"])
N_NULL = 300
MIN_CELLS = 200
MIN_MODULES = 200

LIBS = [
    ("GSM7890839_Expt2_ConfettiRFPr1", "Confetti", "control", "r1"),
    ("GSM7890840_Expt2_ConfettiRFPr2", "Confetti", "control", "r2"),
    ("GSM7890841_Expt2_2wRFP_hetr1", "2w", "het", "r1"),
    ("GSM7890842_Expt2_2wRFP_hetr2", "2w", "het", "r2"),
    ("GSM7890843_Expt2_2wRFP_homr1", "2w", "hom", "r1"),
    ("GSM7890844_Expt2_2wRFP_homr2", "2w", "hom", "r2"),
    ("GSM7890845_Expt2_12wRFP_hetr1", "12w", "het", "r1"),
    ("GSM7890846_Expt2_12wRFP_hetr2", "12w", "het", "r2"),
    ("GSM7890847_Expt2_12wRFP_homr1", "12w", "hom", "r1"),
    ("GSM7890848_Expt2_12wRFP_homr2", "12w", "hom", "r2"),
]

RULES = {
    "question": "does deleting Il1r1 move the Wnt programme in Kras-mutant AT2-derived clones",
    "why_route_A_was_impossible": ("England 2025's Figure S2L-N, the only direct measurement of Axin2 and "
                                   "Il1r1 overlap in the literature, was never deposited: the superseries and "
                                   "both subseries contain zero mentions of ZsGreen and the NCBI link service "
                                   "returns three series for that paper, none holding the sorted libraries"),
    "what_is_asked_instead": ("the untested half of the crosstalk Choi 2020 hypothesised. IL-1 to Wnt in AT2 "
                              "is not established anywhere; the reverse, Wnt inducing IL-1beta, is established "
                              "by Aumiller 2013 and almost never cited"),
    "the_ceiling": ("two libraries per group; under a permutation of four labels into two groups of two there "
                    "are six orderings, so the smallest attainable p is 1/6 and nothing here can be significant. "
                    "The trial computes no p value and reports complete separation or its absence"),
    "R1_unit": "the library; mice per library are not reported in GEO so the mouse-level n is unknown and not assumed",
    "R2_label_corroboration": "Il1r1 must be lower in both homozygous libraries than in both heterozygous ones, checked before any Wnt quantity",
    "R3_readout": f"pseudobulk Wnt target module ({', '.join(WNT)}) as summed counts over summed totals per 10,000",
    "R4_null": f"{N_NULL} random six-gene modules matched on total expression within a factor of two",
    "R5_reading": "complete separation and clearing the null; complete separation only; neither; or R2 failing",
    "register_status": "Descriptive only whatever it finds",
}


def load(stem: str):
    return read_mtx_triplet(RAW / f"{stem}_matrix.mtx.gz",
                            RAW / f"{stem}_features.tsv.gz",
                            RAW / f"{stem}_barcodes.tsv.gz")


def main() -> None:
    rec = RunRecord(OUT / "a2_run_record.json", "A2 Il1r1 dosage against the Wnt programme", RULES)
    profiles, meta = {}, []
    for stem, tp, geno, rep in LIBS:
        for suffix in ("matrix.mtx.gz", "features.tsv.gz", "barcodes.tsv.gz"):
            rec.add_input(RAW / f"{stem}_{suffix}")
        X, var, obs = load(stem)
        total = float(X.sum())
        counts = np.asarray(X.sum(axis=0)).ravel()
        # the shared reader names this column gene_symbol; a first run of this
        # trial looked for "symbol", fell through to the Ensembl id and returned
        # zero for every gene in every library including the controls. That is an
        # impossible table rather than an outcome, so it is a repaired bug and not
        # a moved rule, and it is recorded here rather than quietly fixed.
        assert "gene_symbol" in var.columns, var.columns.tolist()
        sym = list(var["gene_symbol"])
        cpm = pd.Series(counts / total * 1e4, index=sym)
        cpm = cpm.groupby(level=0).sum()
        assert float(cpm.get("Sftpc", 0.0)) > 0, (
            f"{stem}: Sftpc is zero in AT2-derived cells, which means the symbol "
            "lookup is broken rather than the biology surprising")
        profiles[stem] = cpm
        meta.append({"library": stem, "timepoint": tp, "genotype": geno, "replicate": rep,
                     "cells": int(X.shape[0]), "total_counts": int(total),
                     "Il1r1": round(float(cpm.get("Il1r1", 0.0)), 4),
                     "wnt_module": round(float(sum(cpm.get(g, 0.0) for g in WNT)), 4)})
        print(meta[-1])

    md = pd.DataFrame(meta)
    md.to_csv(OUT / "a2_libraries.csv", index=False)
    rec.add_output(OUT / "a2_libraries.csv")

    rows = []
    for tp in ("2w", "12w"):
        sub = md[md.timepoint == tp]
        het = sub[sub.genotype == "het"]
        hom = sub[sub.genotype == "hom"]
        base = {"timepoint": tp, "n_het": len(het), "n_hom": len(hom),
                "il1r1_het": list(het.Il1r1), "il1r1_hom": list(hom.Il1r1),
                "wnt_het": list(het.wnt_module), "wnt_hom": list(hom.wnt_module)}
        if sub.cells.min() < MIN_CELLS:
            rows.append({**base, "reading": "not computable", "why": f"U1, a library below {MIN_CELLS} cells"})
            continue
        # R2: corroborate the genotype labels on Il1r1 itself
        r2_ok = bool(hom.Il1r1.max() < het.Il1r1.min())
        base["R2_corroborated"] = r2_ok
        if not r2_ok:
            rows.append({**base, "reading": "not computable",
                         "why": ("U2, Il1r1 is not lower in both homozygous libraries than in both "
                                 "heterozygous ones, so the genotype labels cannot be verified from "
                                 "three-prime counts and the contrast is refused")})
            print(f"\n{tp}: R2 FAILED  het {list(het.Il1r1)}  hom {list(hom.Il1r1)}")
            continue

        # R4: the matched-module null, built on the same libraries
        pooled = sum(profiles[s] for s in sub.library)
        wnt_level = float(sum(pooled.get(g, 0.0) for g in WNT))
        cand = pooled[(~pooled.index.isin(NAMED)) & (pooled > 0)]
        rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
        names = np.array(cand.index)
        vals = cand.values
        target = np.array([float(pooled.get(g, 0.0)) for g in WNT])
        nulls = []
        for _ in range(N_NULL * 4):
            if len(nulls) >= N_NULL:
                break
            pick = []
            ok = True
            for t in target:
                m = np.flatnonzero((vals >= t / 2) & (vals <= t * 2))
                if len(m) == 0:
                    ok = False
                    break
                pick.append(names[int(rng.choice(m))])
            if not ok or len(set(pick)) < len(target):
                continue
            hv = [float(sum(profiles[s].get(g, 0.0) for g in pick)) for s in het.library]
            ov = [float(sum(profiles[s].get(g, 0.0) for g in pick)) for s in hom.library]
            denom = np.mean(hv)
            if denom > 0:
                nulls.append((np.mean(ov) - np.mean(hv)) / denom)
        nulls = np.array(nulls)
        if len(nulls) < MIN_MODULES:
            rows.append({**base, "reading": "not computable", "why": f"U3, only {len(nulls)} modules"})
            continue

        obs = (hom.wnt_module.mean() - het.wnt_module.mean()) / het.wnt_module.mean()
        lo, hi = float(np.percentile(nulls, 2.5)), float(np.percentile(nulls, 97.5))
        clears = bool(obs < lo or obs > hi)
        sep = bool(hom.wnt_module.max() < het.wnt_module.min() or
                   hom.wnt_module.min() > het.wnt_module.max())
        base.update({"wnt_relative_change": round(float(obs), 4),
                     "null_lo": round(lo, 4), "null_hi": round(hi, 4),
                     "null_modules": len(nulls),
                     "percentile": round(float((nulls < obs).mean()), 4),
                     "complete_separation": sep, "clears_null": clears})
        if sep and clears:
            base.update(reading="the Wnt programme moves with Il1r1 dosage",
                        why="R5a, complete separation and outside the matched-module null")
        elif sep:
            base.update(reading="not distinguishable from matched random modules",
                        why="R5b, complete separation but within what random modules do at this sample size")
        else:
            base.update(reading="no movement detectable at this sample size", why="R5c, no complete separation")
        rows.append(base)
        print(f"\n{tp}: R2 ok. Wnt het {list(het.wnt_module)} hom {list(hom.wnt_module)}  "
              f"rel {obs:+.3f}  null 95% [{lo:+.3f},{hi:+.3f}]  sep={sep} clears={clears}")
        print("   ->", base["reading"])

    res = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    res.to_csv(OUT / "a2_readings.csv", index=False)
    rec.add_output(OUT / "a2_readings.csv")
    rec.set("readings", res.to_dict("records"))
    rec.set("smallest_attainable_p", 1 / 6)
    rec.set("no_p_value_computed", True)
    rec.finish()
    print("\n" + md.to_string(index=False))
    print("\n" + res.to_string(index=False))
    print("\nwrote", OUT)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
