#!/usr/bin/env python
"""Trial W1: per-animal differential expression of the alveolar macrophage (aMAC)
population by repair phase, GSE262927.

Proposal W1 of the paper-1 plan, narrowed on the owner's instruction of
2026-09-22 to the one myeloid population that clears the cell floor in every
arm. A whole-compartment pseudobulk reads composition and state together (C158,
C161); inside one population it reads state. Everything below was fixed from
sample metadata and tracked cell-count tables only, before any expression count
was read.

Design facts found while writing these rules (metadata only)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- aMAC cells per animal clear 50 in 4 of 8 active-repair animals (one at 11 dpi,
  two at 19, one at 25; the 6 dpi animals hold 13 each), 11 of 12 resolution
  animals and all 3 long-term animals. "Active repair" here therefore means late
  active repair, 11 to 25 dpi, when the pool is being rebuilt (C12, C15).
- Three animals are Ki67Cre/Cre (EEM-scRNA-125 and -127 at 42 dpi, -236 at 90 dpi).
- Infection round: the three long-term animals share round 2022-12-06 with the
  four 90 dpi animals and no other; resolution against long-term is therefore
  identified only inside that round, 90 against 366 dpi.
- Age: the 366 dpi animals are about nine months older than the 90 dpi animals
  and no aged uninjured animal exists in the deposit. Ageing alone changes
  alveolar macrophages (McQuattie-Pimentel et al. 2021, doi:10.1172/JCI140299).

Rules, frozen before any count is read
--------------------------------------
R1  Unit: the animal. Cells: the deposited label "aMAC" among trial 11's myeloid
    cells (myeloid_focus/tables/myeloid_cell_metadata.csv), annotated cohort.
    An animal enters only with at least 50 aMAC cells. Pseudobulk = sum of raw
    counts per animal; duplicate gene symbols are summed.
R2  Genotype: Ki67Cre/Cre animals are excluded from the primary analysis,
    because a homozygous knock-in at Mki67 may alter Mki67 itself and the
    reporter dosage differs by round (trial N4); Mki67 is also removed from
    every gene set below.
R3  Arms: active repair = 6 to 25 dpi, injury resolution = 42 and 90 dpi,
    long-term = 366 dpi; baseline (two animals) enters no test. An arm with
    fewer than 3 animals after R1 and R2 stops its contrast.
R4  Contrasts: A = active repair against injury resolution; B = injury
    resolution against long-term. Genes: at least 10 counts in at least 3
    animals of the contrast.
R5  Gene level: pydeseq2 0.5.4, design ~ batch + sex + arm (batch = infection
    round), Wald test on the arm coefficient, Benjamini-Hochberg; eight CPUs.
    A design that is not of full rank stops its contrast.
R6  Machinery gate, per fit: in the same fit, Xist must be higher in female
    than in male animals and at least one of Ddx3y, Eif2s3y, Kdm5d, Uty lower,
    each at padj < 0.05. A fit that fails the gate is unreadable. This checks
    the pipeline, not phase biology; C156 showed that a gate taken from the
    source paper's phase narrative can fail for reasons of timing.
R7  Gene sets, pre-registered, from the on-disk MSigDB 2024.1.Mm files (sha256
    in the record), Mki67 removed, 10 to 500 genes after intersection, else
    "not tested": HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS, HALLMARK_G2M_CHECKPOINT,
    GOBP_MITOTIC_DNA_REPLICATION, GOBP_CELL_CYCLE_DNA_REPLICATION (the
    proliferation readout of C158 and C161), GOBP_ARGININE_METABOLIC_PROCESS and
    GOBP_ORNITHINE_METABOLIC_PROCESS (a proxy for the ARG1 and ornithine circuit
    of Yadav et al. 2025, doi:10.1172/JCI188734, whose own list is not on disk).
R8  Set statistic: CAMERA (Wu and Smyth 2012, doi:10.1093/nar/gks461) on log2
    CPM under the same design, ordinary least squares t per gene, the set's
    inter-gene correlation estimated from residuals as a variance inflation
    factor, competitive two-sided test, Benjamini-Hochberg over the six sets of
    one contrast. Chosen because it respects inter-gene correlation, and because
    an animal-label permutation respecting round has 70 arrangements in A and
    20 in B, so it could not reach p < 0.05 in B at all.
R9  Reading, fixed now. Nothing is Validated (one deposit, arms of 3 to 8).
    Gene level: Descriptive only. A set at FDR < 0.05 in A: Exploratory. A set
    at FDR < 0.05 in B: Descriptive only, and its reading must name age and the
    within-round sex imbalance as alternatives to injury. A proliferation set
    higher in resolution than long-term would say the C158 signal has an aMAC
    component, or an age effect; its absence bounds nothing at 3 animals, so
    only a positive result is read (the lesson of C159). W1 uses the same
    animals as C158 and cannot confirm it independently.

Outputs (w1_amac_pseudobulk_de/): w1_units.csv, w1_gate.csv, w1_sets.csv,
w1_de_top_<A|B>.csv, w1_summary.md, w1_run_record.json; full DE tables as
csv.gz (ignored, regenerable).
"""
from __future__ import annotations

import time
from pathlib import Path

import anndata as ad
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from scipy import sparse, stats

HERE = Path(__file__).resolve().parent
REPO = HERE.parents[2]
sys.path.insert(0, str(HERE))
sys.path.insert(0, str(REPO / "Thesis" / "gate1_04_sikkema_2023_hlca" / "trials"))
sys.path.insert(0, str(REPO / "analysis" / "scripts"))
from trial_utils import RunRecord, df_to_markdown  # noqa: E402
from pipeline_utils import SERIES_DIRS  # noqa: E402
import gsea_utils as gu  # noqa: E402

MOUSE = SERIES_DIRS["GSE262927"]
OUT = HERE / "w1_amac_pseudobulk_de"
CELL_FLOOR, ARM_FLOOR, GENE_MIN_COUNTS, GENE_MIN_ANIMALS = 50, 3, 10, 3
SET_MIN, SET_MAX = 10, 500
ARM_OF_DAY = {6: "active", 11: "active", 19: "active", 25: "active", 42: "resolution", 90: "resolution", 366: "longterm"}
CONTRASTS = {"A": ("active", "resolution"), "B": ("resolution", "longterm")}
SEX_UP_F, SEX_UP_M = "Xist", ["Ddx3y", "Eif2s3y", "Kdm5d", "Uty"]
SETS = {"hallmark": ["HALLMARK_E2F_TARGETS", "HALLMARK_G2M_CHECKPOINT"],
        "gobp": ["GOBP_MITOTIC_DNA_REPLICATION", "GOBP_CELL_CYCLE_DNA_REPLICATION",
                 "GOBP_ARGININE_METABOLIC_PROCESS", "GOBP_ORNITHINE_METABOLIC_PROCESS"]}
GMT = {"hallmark": gu.MSIGDB / "mh.all.v2024.1.Mm.symbols.gmt", "gobp": gu.MSIGDB / "m5.go.bp.v2024.1.Mm.symbols.gmt"}
DESIGN = "~ batch + sex + arm"
RULES = {
    "R1_unit": "animal; deposited label aMAC among trial 11 myeloid cells; >= 50 aMAC cells; raw-count pseudobulk, duplicate symbols summed",
    "R2_genotype": "Ki67Cre/Cre animals excluded; Mki67 removed from every set",
    "R3_arms": {"active": [6, 11, 19, 25], "resolution": [42, 90], "longterm": [366], "baseline": "no test", "arm_floor": ARM_FLOOR},
    "R4_contrasts": {"A": "active vs resolution", "B": "resolution vs longterm", "genes": ">= 10 counts in >= 3 animals of the contrast"},
    "R5_gene_level": "pydeseq2 0.5.4, design ~ batch + sex + arm, Wald on arm, BH, 8 cpus; rank-deficient design stops the contrast",
    "R6_gate": "same fit: Xist F > M and >= 1 of Ddx3y, Eif2s3y, Kdm5d, Uty M > F, padj < 0.05; failure makes the fit unreadable",
    "R7_sets": {**SETS, "size": "10 to 500 after intersection, Mki67 removed, else not tested"},
    "R8_set_statistic": "CAMERA on log2 CPM, OLS t, residual inter-gene correlation VIF, competitive two-sided, BH over the six sets per contrast",
    "R9_reading": "nothing Validated; gene level Descriptive only; set FDR < 0.05 in A Exploratory, in B Descriptive only naming age and sex; only a positive B result is read; not independent of C158",
}


def log(msg: str) -> None:
    print(time.strftime("%H:%M:%S"), msg, flush=True)


def animals_and_pseudobulk(rec: RunRecord):
    src = MOUSE / "processed" / "final_clustered.h5ad"
    my_path = MOUSE / "myeloid_focus" / "tables" / "myeloid_cell_metadata.csv"
    meta_path = MOUSE / "myeloid_focus" / "batch_sensitivity" / "tables" / "sample_infection_round.csv"
    for p in (src, my_path, meta_path):
        rec.add_input(p)
    a = ad.read_h5ad(src, backed="r")
    obs = a.obs
    genes = a.var["gene_symbol"].astype(str).to_numpy() if "gene_symbol" in a.var else a.var_names.astype(str).to_numpy()
    n_cells = a.n_obs
    annotated = obs["has_author_metadata"].astype(str).isin(["True", "true"]).to_numpy()
    sample = obs["sample_id"].astype(str).to_numpy()
    day = pd.to_numeric(obs["sacrifice_day"], errors="coerce").to_numpy()
    obs_index = obs.index.astype(str)
    a.file.close()

    my = pd.read_csv(my_path)
    amac = np.zeros(n_cells, dtype=bool)
    idx = obs_index.get_indexer(my.loc[my["label"] == "aMAC", "cell_id"].astype(str))
    assert (idx >= 0).all(), f"{int((idx < 0).sum())} aMAC cell ids are not in the object; refusing"
    amac[idx] = True
    amac &= annotated
    meta = pd.read_csv(meta_path).set_index("sample_id")

    units, animals = [], []
    group_of_cell = np.full(n_cells, -1, dtype=np.int64)
    for s in sorted(set(sample[amac])):
        m = amac & (sample == s)
        d = int(np.nanmedian(day[m]))
        n = int(m.sum())
        geno = str(meta.loc[s, "genotype"])
        homo = "Cre/Cre" in geno
        arm = ARM_OF_DAY.get(d, "baseline")
        keep = n >= CELL_FLOOR and not homo and arm != "baseline"
        units.append({"animal": s, "day": d, "arm": arm, "aMAC_cells": n, "round": str(meta.loc[s, "round"]),
                      "sex": str(meta.loc[s, "sex"]), "genotype": geno,
                      "kept": keep, "reason": "" if keep else ("baseline" if arm == "baseline" else "Ki67Cre/Cre" if homo else "below 50 cells")})
        if keep:
            group_of_cell[m] = len(animals)
            animals.append(s)
    units = pd.DataFrame(units)
    log(f"pseudobulk over {len(animals)} animals from {int((group_of_cell >= 0).sum()):,} aMAC cells")
    pb = gu.pseudobulk_from_h5ad_counts(src, group_of_cell, len(animals))
    uniq, inv = np.unique(genes, return_inverse=True)
    collapse = sparse.csr_matrix((np.ones(len(genes)), (np.arange(len(genes)), inv)), shape=(len(genes), len(uniq)))
    pb = np.asarray(sparse.csr_matrix(pb) @ collapse).astype(np.int64) if sparse.issparse(pb) else (pb @ collapse).astype(np.int64)
    counts = pd.DataFrame(np.asarray(pb), index=animals, columns=uniq)
    kept = units[units.kept].set_index("animal").loc[animals]
    sample_meta = pd.DataFrame({"arm": kept["arm"], "batch": "r" + kept["round"].str.replace("-", ""), "sex": kept["sex"]}, index=animals)
    return units, counts, sample_meta


def design_matrix(meta: pd.DataFrame):
    from formulaic import model_matrix
    X = model_matrix(DESIGN, meta)
    return X.to_numpy(dtype=float), list(X.columns)


def camera(logcpm: np.ndarray, X: np.ndarray, k: int, genes: np.ndarray, sets: dict[str, set]) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """CAMERA-style competitive test. logcpm: animals by genes. k: column of the arm coefficient."""
    n, p = X.shape
    beta, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(X, logcpm, rcond=None)
    resid = logcpm - X @ beta
    df = n - p
    s2 = (resid ** 2).sum(0) / df
    xtx_inv = np.linalg.inv(X.T @ X)
    t = beta[k] / np.sqrt(np.maximum(s2 * xtx_inv[k, k], 1e-300))
    G = len(t)
    var_t = np.var(t, ddof=1)
    U = resid / np.maximum(np.linalg.norm(resid, axis=0), 1e-300)
    rows = []
    for name, members in sets.items():
        hit = np.isin(genes, list(members))
        m = int(hit.sum())
        if m < SET_MIN or m > SET_MAX:
            rows.append({"set": name, "size": m, "tested": False})
            continue
        s = U[:, hit].sum(1)
        rho = (float(s @ s) - m) / (m * (m - 1))
        vif = 1 + (m - 1) * rho
        diff = t[hit].mean() - t[~hit].mean()
        T = diff / np.sqrt(var_t * (vif / m + 1 / (G - m)))
        rows.append({"set": name, "size": m, "tested": True, "mean_t_set": float(t[hit].mean()), "mean_t_rest": float(t[~hit].mean()),
                     "inter_gene_corr": rho, "vif": vif, "camera_t": float(T), "p": float(2 * stats.t.sf(abs(T), G - 2)),
                     "direction": "up in first arm" if diff > 0 else "down in first arm"})
    out = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    tested = out["tested"]
    out.loc[tested, "fdr"] = stats.false_discovery_control(out.loc[tested, "p"].to_numpy(), method="bh") if tested.any() else np.nan
    return out


def main() -> int:
    from pydeseq2.dds import DeseqDataSet
    from pydeseq2.default_inference import DefaultInference
    from pydeseq2.ds import DeseqStats

    OUT.mkdir(exist_ok=True)
    rec = RunRecord(OUT / "w1_run_record.json", "W1 aMAC pseudobulk differential expression by phase, per animal", RULES)
    rec.set("gene_set_files", {k: gu.gene_set_facts(v) for k, v in GMT.items()})
    import importlib.metadata as md_
    rec.set("versions", {p: md_.version(p) for p in ("pydeseq2", "formulaic", "anndata", "numpy", "pandas", "scipy")})
    units, counts, meta = animals_and_pseudobulk(rec)
    units.to_csv(OUT / "w1_units.csv", index=False)
    rec.add_output(OUT / "w1_units.csv")
    rec.set("animals_per_arm", meta["arm"].value_counts().to_dict())

    gmt = {c: gu.read_gmt(p) for c, p in GMT.items()}
    sets = {s: gmt[c][s] - {"Mki67"} for c, names in SETS.items() for s in names}
    inference = DefaultInference(n_cpus=8)
    gate_rows, set_tables, verdict = [], [], {}
    for tag, (first, second) in CONTRASTS.items():
        sub = meta[meta["arm"].isin([first, second])].copy()
        n1, n2 = int((sub.arm == first).sum()), int((sub.arm == second).sum())
        if min(n1, n2) < ARM_FLOOR:
            verdict[tag] = f"stopped: {n1} against {n2} animals"
            continue
        sub["arm"] = pd.Categorical(sub["arm"], categories=[second, first])
        X, cols = design_matrix(sub)
        if np.linalg.matrix_rank(X) < X.shape[1]:
            verdict[tag] = f"stopped: design {DESIGN} is rank-deficient for these animals"
            continue
        c = counts.loc[sub.index]
        keep = (c >= GENE_MIN_COUNTS).sum(0) >= GENE_MIN_ANIMALS
        c = c.loc[:, keep]
        log(f"{tag}: {n1} {first} against {n2} {second}, {c.shape[1]:,} genes")
        dds = DeseqDataSet(counts=c, metadata=sub, design=DESIGN, inference=inference, quiet=True)
        dds.deseq2()
        res = {}
        for name, contrast in (("arm", ["arm", first, second]), ("sex", ["sex", "F", "M"])):
            ds = DeseqStats(dds, contrast=contrast, inference=inference, quiet=True)
            ds.summary()
            res[name] = ds.results_df
        sx = res["sex"]
        f_ok = SEX_UP_F in sx.index and sx.loc[SEX_UP_F, "padj"] < 0.05 and sx.loc[SEX_UP_F, "log2FoldChange"] > 0
        y = [g for g in SEX_UP_M if g in sx.index and sx.loc[g, "padj"] < 0.05 and sx.loc[g, "log2FoldChange"] < 0]
        gate = bool(f_ok and y)
        gate_rows.append({"contrast": tag, "xist_log2fc_F_vs_M": float(sx.loc[SEX_UP_F, "log2FoldChange"]) if SEX_UP_F in sx.index else np.nan,
                          "xist_padj": float(sx.loc[SEX_UP_F, "padj"]) if SEX_UP_F in sx.index else np.nan,
                          "y_genes_clearing": ",".join(y), "gate": gate})
        de = res["arm"].sort_values("pvalue")
        de.to_csv(OUT / f"w1_de_{tag}.csv.gz", compression="gzip")
        top = de[de["padj"] < 0.05] if (de["padj"] < 0.05).sum() >= 30 else de.head(30)
        top.round(6).to_csv(OUT / f"w1_de_top_{tag}.csv")
        rec.add_output(OUT / f"w1_de_top_{tag}.csv")
        lc = gu.log_cpm(c.to_numpy())
        k = next(i for i, cname in enumerate(cols) if cname.startswith("arm"))
        cam = camera(lc, X, k, c.columns.to_numpy(), sets)
        cam.insert(0, "contrast", tag)
        set_tables.append(cam)
        verdict[tag] = {"animals": [n1, n2], "genes_tested": int(c.shape[1]), "genes_padj05": int((de["padj"] < 0.05).sum()),
                        "gate": gate, "sets_fdr05": int((cam.get("fdr", pd.Series(dtype=float)) < 0.05).sum()) if gate else "unreadable"}
        log(f"{tag}: gate {'passes' if gate else 'FAILS'}; {verdict[tag]['genes_padj05']} genes at padj < 0.05")
    gate_df = pd.DataFrame(gate_rows)
    gate_df.to_csv(OUT / "w1_gate.csv", index=False)
    sets_df = pd.concat(set_tables, ignore_index=True) if set_tables else pd.DataFrame()
    sets_df.to_csv(OUT / "w1_sets.csv", index=False)
    for p in ("w1_gate.csv", "w1_sets.csv"):
        rec.add_output(OUT / p)
    rec.set("verdicts", verdict)

    md = ["# Trial W1: aMAC pseudobulk differential expression by phase, per animal", "",
          "Generated by `w1_amac_pseudobulk_de.py`; rules R1 to R9 in the docstring and the run record, frozen",
          "before any count was read. Unit: the animal. Nothing here is Validated.", "",
          "## Animals", "",
          df_to_markdown(units.drop(columns=["genotype"]), index=False), "",
          "## Machinery gate (R6)", "", df_to_markdown(gate_df.round(6), index=False) if len(gate_df) else "no fit", "",
          "## Pre-registered sets, CAMERA (R8)", "",
          df_to_markdown(sets_df.round(4), index=False) if len(sets_df) else "no contrast computed", "",
          "## Verdicts", ""]
    for tag, v in verdict.items():
        md.append(f"- **{tag}** ({CONTRASTS[tag][0]} against {CONTRASTS[tag][1]}): {v}")
    md += ["", "## Reading under R9", "",
           "Gene-level results are Descriptive only. A set at FDR < 0.05 in contrast A is Exploratory; in contrast B it is",
           "Descriptive only and confounded with age (about nine months) and, within round 2022-12-06, with sex. Only a",
           "positive B result is read; an absence at three animals bounds nothing. W1 shares its animals with C158 and",
           "cannot confirm it. Statuses proposed for the register await the owner's retain or reject."]
    (OUT / "w1_summary.md").write_text("\n".join(md) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    rec.add_output(OUT / "w1_summary.md")
    rec.finish()
    log("done")
    return 0


if __name__ == "__main__":
    raise SystemExit(main())
