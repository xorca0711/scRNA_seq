# Repository and research-question review — 22 September 2026

**Verdict:** the biological theme is worthwhile, and several observations are useful. The repository does not yet operationalize a test of productive repair versus persistent remodelling. Its principal weakness is the gap between the quantity computed and the biological conclusion attached to it. Some of that gap is acknowledged; several consequential instances remain in the current claims and question-first narrative.

Review baseline: commit `90285d74adf668c252e3954b1ed98c748bea787d`, after W1. This review scans the register and decision history, examines the major analysis branches, and checks selected evidence and implementations in depth. It is not a raw-data reproduction of all 164 claims. Three additional agents independently examined ligand claims, retained statistics, and research-question validity. Existing claims, statuses, frozen trials and owner decisions were not edited.

The audit calculations, input hashes and exact scope are in [audit_checks.json](audit_checks.json), generated by [check_artifacts.py](check_artifacts.py), and [ligand_checks.json](ligand_checks.json). New diagnostic comparisons below are explicitly post hoc, not new validated claims.

## 2. Findings that should change the next decision

### 2.1 High priority: the purported epithelial-to-fibroblast scan admits mural senders

C12 builds its object from **both epithelial and stromal cells**, then selects fibroblast targets while excluding only fibroblasts/myofibroblasts from the sender column. That still permits **pericytes and smooth-muscle cells as senders**. C14 reuses the object and the same exclusion. The implemented contrast is broader than the declared epithelial-to-fibroblast contrast.

This is visible in the existing output: donor `021I` has its top three pairs from **Pericyte to Myofibroblast**. Across the stored table, **1,082/8,709 donor-by-pair winners (12.4%)** and **106/390 donor top-15 positions (27.2%)** have pericyte or smooth-muscle senders, affecting 13 donors. These conservative counts exclude mesothelial senders. It is particularly consequential when the interpretation concerns abundant matrix ligands allegedly coming from epithelium. C14 then drops sender identity from its saved ranking, limiting retrospective verification.

The eligibility floor also uses all stromal cells rather than the actual fibroblast targets. Four admitted donors have fewer than 50 fibroblasts/myofibroblasts: `158I` (43), `160C` (47), `209I` (33) and `388C` (44). This explains the difference between the 26-donor C12 scan and the 22-donor E6 contrast. Counts and source hashes are recorded in the ligand audit JSON.

**Affected:** C80–C84 and C111–C115, especially the epithelial scope of the resource-comparison interpretation. Database dependence may remain real; the current outputs do not establish it for the promised sender population. No stored EGFR winner has a pericyte or smooth-muscle sender, so this does not demonstrate that AREG loses its EGFR ranking.

**Improvement:** a separate corrected pass must use an explicit epithelial sender allowlist, require the cell floor in the actual sender and target populations, preserve source/target labels in every output, and regenerate the resource comparisons. Keep the original trial as the historical result.

Evidence: [C12 implementation](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/c12_cellchatdb_full_resource_scan.py), lines 159–160 and 249–250; [C12 donor-pair table](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/c12_cellchatdb_full_resource_scan/c12_per_donor_pairs.csv), rows for donor `021I`; [C14 implementation](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/c14_does_the_ranking_depend_on_the_database.py), lines 144–150.

### 2.2 High priority: C161's statistical caveat also applies to held-out validation

The retained decision correctly narrows C161 to reproducible directions and warns that the discovery nulls sample genes rather than donors. However, **the held-out cohort is evaluated using the same preranked GSEA gene-permutation procedure**. Sample-level pseudobulk construction and a sample-level enrichment null are different things.

The stored counts reproduce: **254 discovery sets passed; 62 meet the recorded replication criterion**, comprising 8 AT2, 3 fibroblast and 51 macrophage sets. Independent cohorts strengthen the evidence that these directions repeat. They do not automatically calibrate both cohorts' significance tests or remove shared cell-mixture and ambient-RNA effects. Nor are 62 overlapping gene sets 62 independent biological mechanisms.

**Improvement:** retain the observed cross-cohort concordance, explicitly extend the null caveat to validation, and re-evaluate frozen candidate sets using a verified correlation-aware method or donor-level resampling appropriate to the design. Examine leading-edge overlap and subtype composition. The small AT2 arm and its mesenchymal/immune signals should remain the least persuasive biological interpretation.

Evidence: [C161](../../../CLAIMS.md), line 51; [G2 implementation](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/g2_gsea_ipf.py), lines 179, 228 and 251–257; [shared enrichment implementation](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/gsea_utils.py), `run_prerank`; [G2 replication table](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/g2_gsea_ipf/g2_replication.csv). The correlation problem is the methodological motivation of [Wu and Smyth's CAMERA paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3458527/).

### 2.3 High priority for method validity: W1 did not run standard CAMERA

The function calls itself “CAMERA-style,” while the rules and C163 call it CAMERA. It estimates inter-gene correlation from residuals but evaluates the set statistic using **gene-count degrees of freedom, `G - 2`**. The official implementation uses the residual degrees of freedom, capped by `G - 2`, when correlation is estimated. W1 also substitutes ordinary OLS statistics and global variance for the official moderated/transformed statistics and pooled variance calculation.

These are substantive implementation differences, not naming details. **They do not prove the no-hit result is wrong.** Correcting degrees of freedom alone would increase the recorded p-values, so cannot create a positive result. A full replacement still needs verification.

**Improvement:** run a pinned limma implementation, explicitly document estimated versus fixed correlation, and compare a small known example before interpreting a new biological run. Until then, describe C163 as the result of the custom approximation. The sex-gene gate checks part of the count-analysis machinery; it does not validate pathway sensitivity or the phase contrast.

Evidence: [W1 function](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/w1_amac_pseudobulk_de.py), lines 184–213; [stored set results](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/w1_amac_pseudobulk_de/w1_sets.csv); [official limma source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bioc/limma/master/R/geneset-camera.R), the `df.camera`, moderated-statistic and set-variance calculations.

### 2.5 C37's gate is poor, but its directional lead survives a diagnostic check

The original numbers and test reproduce: epithelial detection **0.3363**, “myeloid” **0.2147**, 11 donors, exact paired p = **0.001953125**. However, the tracked cross-tab shows that only **24.8%** of the original myeloid gate's cells with nonmissing deposited type labels are annotated Myeloid; **60.4%** are T/NK. In tumour lung alone, reconstructing the original gate gives 4,289 cells: 974 annotated Myeloid, 1,898 T/NK and 701 without a deposited type, plus other types. This follows from permissive any-marker detection and overwrite priority in the gating code.

Importantly, replacing the gates with deposited major cell types, retaining the same AREG cache and 50-cell floor, **preserves the direction in all 10 eligible donors**: medians **0.4591 versus 0.2715**, exact p = **0.001953125**. This is a post hoc sensitivity check with no depth adjustment, not a new confirmatory result.

**Improvement:** qualify what the original “Validated” number validates, and formalize the annotation-based sensitivity analysis. The comparison does not show that epithelium is the exclusive source, the largest total tissue source, or the functionally relevant source; per-cell detection lacks abundance, secretion, processing and spatial information.

Evidence: [E1 gating and test](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/e1_human_luad_ligand_sources.py), lines 149–163 and 192–203; [deposited-label cross-tab](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/e1_human_luad_ligand_sources/e1_gate_vs_deposited_celltype.csv); exact inputs and diagnostic calculations in the audit script/JSON.

### 2.6 C105/C107 exclude more than their measurement can exclude

The Epcam trial's decision rule measures the fraction of cells with detectable transcript, often near saturation. Failure to observe a ten-percentage-point detection deficit does **not** exclude lower transcript abundance among expressing cells. The companion table also records mean normalized expression, with increases in the sampled transitional-state contrasts; that is additional evidence against a broad average decrease in those sampled states. Neither measurement establishes that any surface dimming in the actual escaping cells “would have to be post-transcriptional.” The study has not established surface dimming in those cells themselves.

**Improvement:** narrow the result to no consistent large detection-fraction deficit under the tested rule, supported by the stored mean-expression direction. Assess those means with depth controls and retain the distinction between companion states and actual sorted contaminants. Surface staining is required to establish dimming, and a targeted perturbation would be needed to test shedding. Also correct C106's blanket 0.89–1.00 range: an evaluable library reports 0.6674 versus 0.6456.

Evidence: [C13 summary](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/c13_epcam_transcript_in_the_transitional_state/c13_summary.md), [state-level detection and means](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/c13_epcam_transcript_in_the_transitional_state/c13_by_library_and_state.csv); C105–C107 in [the register](../../../CLAIMS.md).

### 2.7 A significance threshold is not a bound on undetected biology

E6 computes `1.96 / sqrt(n - 1)` and describes the result, approximately rho 0.43 for 22 donors, as a detectable effect size. That is an approximate significance threshold for an observed statistic, **not an exclusion bound or a conventional 80%-power calculation**. RQ A2 inherits the stronger language.

For scale only, a Fisher-z approximation at n = 22 and observed rho 0.3484 gives an interval of roughly **−0.09 to 0.67** and an 80%-power correlation around **0.57**. These audit calculations are explicitly not a formal Spearman confidence interval or power analysis. The present result cannot rule out biologically meaningful coupling. Conversely, ligand and receptor transcript amounts need not covary across donors even when local signalling occurs.

Similarly, C156's failed G2M gate establishes that its enrichment criterion was not met, not that the active-repair compartment “is not more proliferative.”

**Improvement:** use effect estimates with appropriate donor-level uncertainty and design-specific power simulations; reserve equivalence language for an explicit margin and test. Evidence: [E6 function](../../../Thesis/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/e6_donor_level_axis_coupling.py), lines 180–184; [RQ A2](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md), lines 103–106; [C156](../../../CLAIMS.md), line 46.

## 3. Is the research question misguided?

**As a motivating theme, no. As a question the current combined analyses can answer, it is underidentified.** The datasets span different species, ages, injuries, genotypes, sorting schemes and disease stages. That diversity can generate hypotheses, but does not itself create a comparison between successful and failed repair. The analyses lack a shared, independently measured repair outcome attached to the molecular samples. Late collection is not proof of recovery; persistent transcription is not proof of pathology; fibrosis and established cancer are not interchangeable failures of one repair process.

The project needs to define what would count as productive repair: mature AT1 contribution, restored alveolar structure/function, resolution of a transitional compartment, or another explicit outcome. These are not equivalent. Molecular states should be compared against that outcome, rather than used simultaneously to define it and explain it.

### A1: useful molecular question, misleading fate interpretation

The most serious conceptual error is equating **AT2 chromatin closure with arrest** and retained AT2 accessibility with reversibility. Productive progression from AT2 through a transitional state into AT1 can itself require losing AT2 identity. A transient state can disappear through differentiation, death or replacement; it need not reverse into AT2. The branch README explicitly says closing favours arrest, which the measured accessibility difference does not establish. [A1](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md), lines 34–72; [branch interpretation](../../../Thesis/gate1_02_choi_2020/datp_epigenetics/README.md), line 123. The original DATP study includes maturation toward AT1, not just return to AT2. [Choi et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487779/).

**GSE309751 would not directly test cellular reversibility.** Different mice at 14 and 49 days, bulk mixtures and thresholded peak calls cannot distinguish reopening in the same cells from altered composition, selection or replacement. Peak absence also depends on sequencing and peak-calling sensitivity. Its valid role is an exploratory test of time-associated bulk peak detection at frozen loci, with the scope stated before analysis. Animal replication is valuable but does not repair a mismatch between assay and question.

There are three additional issues in retained C131/C133:

- The uninjured comparator is **7-week Cebpa mutant**, not healthy wild type. A similar AT2-program loss there can be genuine genetically induced plasticity; it is not, by itself, proof that the statistic is defective. The source study explicitly examines CEBPA-dependent plasticity. [Hassan and Chen 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-48632-3).
- The reported reductions **−37.8%, −18.3%, −15.5%** are background-centered contrasts: `(observed difference − matched-set null mean) / reference baseline`. The corresponding raw differences are **−31.8%, −16.0%, −8.5%**. Calling the adjusted numbers literal accessibility losses hides a meaningful distinction.
- C133's interval is constructed from reference-only random cell splits. It is not an animal-level confidence interval, nor a demonstrated confidence interval covering all uncertainty in the transitional group, background offset and denominator. It should not determine future biological sample size by itself.

Evidence: [M3 readings](../../../Thesis/gate1_02_choi_2020/datp_epigenetics/trials/m3_one_instrument_and_the_right_null/m3_readings.csv); [M3 implementation](../../../Thesis/gate1_02_choi_2020/datp_epigenetics/trials/m3_one_instrument_and_the_right_null.py), lines 219 and 240–263; audited transformations in the JSON.

**Better question:** which RNA and accessibility changes accompany defined transitional states, and which subsequently associate with AT1 maturation, AT2 restoration or persistent pathological states? The current data address parts of the first clause; fate evidence is a separate requirement.

### A2: split source biology from database sensitivity

“Where does AREG come from?” and “how much do resources alter rankings?” have different measurements and validation criteria. Keep both, but do not let agreement across databases validate communication. The same expression data and scoring mechanism are reused, and the resources overlap in their underlying knowledge. A common-pair-universe comparison would separate coverage differences from ranking differences.

Spatial evidence would improve a local-niche hypothesis, but proximity alone would still not establish ligand processing, receptor activation or functional necessity. Correct the sender bug before investing in the next communication analysis. The blanket assertion that this axis has never been asked about spatially needs a dated literature search or narrower wording.

### A3: the clearest descriptive branch, with an outcome and ageing gap

The long time course and lineage information give this branch strong descriptive value. Retain the per-animal fractions and the limited tracing interpretation. Avoid interpreting relative abundance as an absolute population expansion, or a state detected at one year as evidence that it causes failed repair. The same state need not be the same persisting cells.

W1 does not fully deliver the proposed ARG1/ornithine circuit analysis: it substituted broad GO proxies, and the ornithine set was not tested because only seven genes survived. That is an unfilled component, not a negative answer about the circuit. A receiver-side mesenchymal response and metabolite flux are also absent. [W1 rules/results](../../../Thesis/gate1_01_niethamer_2025/trials/w1_amac_pseudobulk_de.py).

### A4: good experimental question, false binary and inconsistent measurement scope

Wnt-associated maintenance and IL-1-associated transition can be sequential or context-dependent in the same cells. They do not force a choice between two stable opposed subsets. Historical Cre labelling, present transcript detection, current pathway activity and future responsiveness are distinct quantities.

Bulk qPCR enrichment in a sorted lineage supports an association; it does not demonstrate same-cell overlap or the fraction occupying four reporter quadrants. The “both four to seven per cent” sentence applies to GSE145031, while the page's own GSE310539 figure reports Il1r1 detection at **19.3–29.8%**. The transcript still does not become a validated lineage-reporter substitute, but the broad sparse-detection assertion is incorrect.

**Better question:** how do current Wnt activity and IL-1 responsiveness co-occur and change over time within AT2 cells? Specify current versus lineage-history readouts, washout, mouse-level quadrants and a functional endpoint. Qualify “nobody,” “only direct evidence” and “untested anywhere” by a documented search scope. [A4](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md), lines 160–203; [figure table](../../../analysis/figures/rq/rq_a4_detection.csv); C136/C138/C146/C148 in the register.

### A5: a sound specificity warning, not yet a shared developmental-program result

The two neonatal values are **3.69% in control** and **8.07% in Cebpa mutant**. The larger value should not be presented as ordinary normal development. Control neonatal positivity is already sufficient to refute injury exclusivity of this two-transcript classifier. It does not show that the complete DATP/PATS/ADI programs, regulatory states or fates are identical to developmental states.

The proposed full-module comparison is worthwhile descriptively. However, scoring a maturation module does not “hold development fixed”: the available design does not provide a replicated age-by-injury factorial comparison. Start within genotype, explicitly distinguish the state definitions, and keep developmental similarity separate from injury-specificity inference. [A5](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md), lines 206–228; [label table](../../../Thesis/gate1_02_choi_2020/datp_epigenetics/trials/m1c_label_at_the_depth_available/m1c_labels.csv).

### A literature gap worth addressing

Gaona et al., published in June 2026, study sustained YAP/TAZ activation, altered alveolar chromatin, persistent remodelling and partial pharmacological rescue. This is directly relevant to A1's mechanistic motivation and deserves comparison before presenting the field's open question. Their mouse multiome pools one male and one female per group, so it is **not** a ready solution to the replication problem. The paper names GSE327686 for mouse and GSE326359 for human genomic data; these are candidates for a design audit, not an instruction to launch another analysis branch. [Gaona et al., JCI Insight](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/198113).

## 5. Repository structure and remaining reproducibility gaps

The structure is understandable but organized around papers and historical trials more than reusable analyses:

- Root files separate the claim ledger, owner decisions, research framing and historical progress. This is useful, but the same interpretation appears in several places and changes do not propagate automatically.
- `analysis/scripts/` contains the original pipeline, figure generators and validator; `Thesis/` contains most later analysis code, trial plans, records and compact evidence tables.
- `raw_data/` contains ignored downloads and caches; large processed objects are also ignored. This keeps Git manageable but means a tracked-table check is not a full reproduction.
- Generic helpers live inside a particular paper's trial directory and are imported across branches through path manipulation. A shared package would make ownership and reuse clearer.

**The main structural gaps are concrete:**

1. **CI validates much less science than its green badge may suggest.** The validator checks links, JSON, unique claim IDs and selected original headline results. It does not reproduce later claim values or verify the implementations above. CI compiles only `analysis/scripts`, while most recent trials live under `Thesis/`.
2. **The local scientific environments do not launch in this session.** Both `.venv` and `.venv-x64` refer to base interpreters unavailable at their recorded paths. The bundled runtime can perform the lightweight validation but lacks the full analysis stack. The public reproduction guide points to the older environment file rather than explaining the newer x64 trial environment and complete dependency order.
3. **Run records are not immutable preregistrations.** `RunRecord` writes the same path again on rerun; general input facts record size and modification time, not content hashes. W1's separate pre-run commit is stronger evidence of prospective specification. Earlier-data-informed corrected passes should be called prospective specifications for the next pass, not independent preregistration of an unseen question.
4. **Narrative provenance exceeds actual coverage.** “Authors' annotations held out of every fitting step” should be limited to the relevant unsupervised fits: G2 and W1 explicitly use deposited labels to construct the groups fitted. E1 uses deposited normalized log2 TPM, so “all reanalysed from raw counts” is also too broad. These are scope corrections, not reasons those analyses cannot be useful.
5. **The headline ledger is not a measure of evidence calibration.** It combines biological propositions, implementation failures, bookkeeping and decision records. Its figure groups by the register's section headings, placing Cardoso claims C65–C84 and C105–C115 under the Choi section. A high negative-row count is not itself evidence of a well-calibrated discovery process.

**Improvement:** introduce a small structured claim manifest with claim kind, estimand, dataset/sample definition, evidence source, numeric field/filter, status and owner-review state. Generate public summaries from it and check their declared numbers. Record shared data and gene-set dependencies explicitly. Freeze code commit, input hashes and environment identity per run. Benchmark custom statistics against reference implementations. Keep historical trial outputs intact.

Evidence: [validator](../../../analysis/scripts/validate_repository.py), especially `check_headline_results`; [CI workflow](../../../.github/workflows/repository-checks.yml); [RunRecord](../../../Thesis/gate1_04_sikkema_2023_hlca/trials/trial_utils.py); [reproduction guide](../../../REPRODUCIBILITY.md); [ledger generator](../../../analysis/scripts/15_claims_ledger_figure.py).

## 6. Plausible improvement sequence

| Order | Work | What it would resolve |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Correct scientific scope in the current narrative: accessibility versus fate, detection versus quantity, significance versus exclusion, and retained versus reviewed | Prevents new analyses being justified by a stronger claim than the evidence supports |
| 2 | Repair and verify the actual measurements: epithelial-only C12/C14, formal annotation sensitivity for C37, reference CAMERA for W1 | Resolves concrete implementation/definition defects before interpreting downstream hypotheses |
| 3 | Reassess C158/C161 with sample-aware designs and calibrated set inference; show leave-one-donor-out stability and subtype contributions | Determines which program directions remain useful priorities |
| 4 | Merge A1 and A5 into an epithelial-state specificity project with frozen full modules, explicit genotype/age contrasts and an independent confirmation cohort | Gives the strongest connected epithelial question a measurable target |
| 5 | Gate new datasets by relevant outcomes, independent samples, cell-state coverage, age controls and raw quantitative assay availability | Avoids another technically sophisticated analysis of an unidentifiable question |
| 6 | Add spatial/functional work only to candidates surviving those steps | Tests local interaction and fate mechanisms at the evidence level they require |

A practical two-level framing would be:

> **Current computational question:** Which epithelial and macrophage program changes reproduce across independent samples after accounting for cell-state composition, genotype and measurement quality?
>
> **Biological follow-up question:** Which of those changes associate with mature AT1 contribution or persistent pathological remodelling in cohorts with independently measured outcomes?

This preserves the intended hypothesis-generation purpose. It makes clear which question existing data can advance and which requires a different design. The most valuable next step is to strengthen a short list of observations and define their discriminating experiments, rather than expand the number of trials or claim rows.

## 7. Decision readiness and review cost

The three bounded agent reviews and deterministic artifact checks are sufficient for this report. A fourth general scan or repeated full audit is unlikely to change its immediate recommendations. Exact aggregate token usage and monetary cost were not available; no cost figure is asserted.

The sender-filter defect, mismatch between W1 and reference CAMERA, use of the same GSEA null in both cohorts, and mismatch between accessibility and cellular reversibility are established by the inspected code and study designs. Another audit is not needed before acknowledging those limitations. Corrected biological results remain unknown until the relevant analysis is rerun.

Before changing a retained scientific decision, use a targeted check tied to that decision:

| Decision | Minimum useful next check |
|---|---|
| Keep epithelial-specific ligand rankings | Correct sender and target-floor filters; compare corrected winners and ranks with the historical output |
| Treat C37 as a validated source comparison | Formalize the deposited-annotation comparison and examine depth sensitivity; the present diagnostic already preserves the direction |
| Claim C161 has calibrated set-level significance | Verify correlation-aware or donor-resampling inference in both cohorts; distinguish candidate confirmation from exploratory screening |
| Interpret C158 as an injury-phase program | Show the estimable, suitably restricted sample contrasts and propagate age/round/sex/genotype limitations |
| Accept W1 as a CAMERA result | Verify against pinned reference limma, then rerun the frozen set analysis; a degrees-of-freedom-only correction cannot produce new hits |
| Choose a reversibility or repair-outcome project | Establish that the proposed dataset or experiment measures the relevant fate/outcome, rather than another proxy |

These are proposed checks, not an invitation to run every analysis automatically. Prioritize the ones attached to the next scientific choice. Owner-retained statuses have been preserved: this report supplies evidence for a reviewable decision, and does not silently replace that decision. No further user confirmation was needed for this read-only investigation or the explicitly requested global preference update.
