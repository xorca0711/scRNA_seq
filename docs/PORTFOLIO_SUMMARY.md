# Public lung data: from attractive claims to defensible questions

**Question.** Which epithelial and macrophage programme changes persist after
accounting for annotation, measurement and sample composition—and which can be
connected to independently measured outcomes?

**Contribution.** This repository reconstructs public mouse and human lung
analyses, exposes their limitations, and preserves a traceable record of claim
corrections. Work includes atlas recovery, sample-level pseudobulks,
ligand-resource auditing and epithelial-state specificity. Source studies own
the datasets; [DEVELOPMENT.md](../DEVELOPMENT.md) records AI assistance and
delegated decisions.

**What changed under scrutiny.** Corrected annotations preserve an unadjusted
AREG source difference, but its primary molecule-matched comparison does not
establish a depth-independent hierarchy. Ligand rankings change with sender
definition and receptor encoding. The historical 62 replicated programme leads
yield zero replications under the primary estimated-correlation CAMERA analysis
and 30 under a fixed-correlation sensitivity; their status is exploratory and
method-sensitive. A source-defined epithelial pilot separates developmental,
injury and genotype contrasts, but only one external animal passes both state
floors. It does not establish replicated confirmation or cellular fate.

**What the work demonstrates.** Reproducible implementation, biological-unit
reasoning, targeted sensitivity analysis, and willingness to revise conclusions.
The generated claim index checks selected numeric fields without claiming to
certify every biological statement.

**IL-1beta context extension.** Completed paired human and IPF niche analyses
separate source/recipient RNA, pathway-model sensitivity and epithelial
programme specificity. Elevated reduced-HPCS scores also occur in repair
and fibrosis; they do not establish malignant identity. The
[shared questions A11–A14](../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a11-shared-plasticity-versus-neoplasia-associated-context)
include PCA, paired program distributions, enrichment and ligand-target
figures, with joint associations and withdrawal outcomes still proposed.

**Next step.** An organoid perturbation dataset offers paired RNA and imaging
measurements. Its biological batches and well mapping must pass the
[dataset gate](NEXT_DATASET_GATE.md) before an outcome model is justified.
