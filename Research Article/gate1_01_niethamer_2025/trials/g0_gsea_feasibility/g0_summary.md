## Reading

Under the unit rule the question is never whether a gene set is enriched in a group of cells, which is
always answerable and never a test, but whether a per-unit pseudobulk ranking carries it. That leaves
4 deposits, and they sort by what they can answer:

- **GSE262927** (Niethamer et al. 2025; animal; 0 dpi: 2; 6 dpi: 2; 11 dpi: 2; 19 dpi: 2; 25 dpi: 2; 42 dpi: 8; 90 dpi: 4; 366 dpi: 3; active repair (6 to 25 dpi): 8; injury resolution (42, 90 dpi): 12; long-term (366 dpi): 3): phase against phase within a compartment (myeloid, capillary, alveolar epithelium); days with three or more animals: 42 dpi, 90 dpi, 366 dpi. Asked by A3; proposals W1, S1. the only mouse deposit with animal-level replication; baseline (2 animals) stays a reference band; the two-animal active-repair days enter only as a pooled phase.
- **GSE136831** (Adams et al. 2020; donor; COPD donors: 18; Control donors: 28; IPF donors: 32): IPF against control within a compartment (AT2 and transitional epithelium, fibroblasts, macrophages). Asked by A2 (C48, C49); A3 in human fibrosis. the largest donor-replicated contrast in reach; per-donor cell floor of trial E6 (50) applies.
- **GSE135893** (Habermann et al. 2020; donor; Control donors: 10; ILD donors: 1; IPF donors: 12; NSIP donors: 3; Sarcoidosis donors: 2; cHP donors: 2): IPF against control within a compartment, as the held-out replication of GSE136831. Asked by A2, A3; proposal V1 names held-out human validation. second fibrosis cohort; a gene set that clears in GSE136831 and not here is not established.
- **GSE131907** (Kim et al. 2020; donor (paired); normal lung donors: 11; tumour lung donors: 11; paired: 10): tumour against paired normal lung within the epithelial compartment. Asked by A2 (C40). paired design; the raw UMI matrix is on disk although trials E1 and E1b read the deposited log2 TPM, so a pseudobulk trial must stream the raw file.
