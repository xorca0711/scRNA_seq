## The test, in full

The endpoint is scored as the mean over a set's members of log2 of counts per
million plus one, using the well's own human total as the denominator, which is the
convention A10 used. Within each unit the endpoint is regressed on an intercept, log2
fibroblast total counts and the epithelial count fraction, and the residual is the
analysed quantity. The Areg well's residual is ranked among its unit's 60 residuals,
ascending, and the four ranks are summed.

| Reference set | Wells per unit | Critical rank sum at alpha 0.05 | Smallest attainable p | Mean percentile required |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Primary, every well | 60, 60, 60, 60 | 64 | 7.72e-08 | 0.267 |
| S1, at least 100,000 fibroblast counts | 32, 48, 31, 57 | 44 | 3.68e-07 | 0.280 |
| S2, the 20 depth-nearest wells | 20, 20, 20, 20 | 22 | 6.25e-06 | 0.275 |

The decision threshold is the rank sum at or below 64 **and** the Areg well below its
unit median in at least three of the four units. The exact size of that joint rule is
0.04903, so it does not inflate the nominal alpha. A rejection says the Areg well is
exceptional among the perturbations in its own unit, in the declared direction. It is
not a comparison against an unperturbed population, and it is conservative when other
wells in the unit act on the same pathway.

S3 repeats the statistic on the unadjusted endpoint. For a supported reading the
primary must clear its threshold and the effect must hold in direction in at least
one of S1 and S2, because the depth asymmetry is known in advance rather than
discovered afterwards.

## The five changes stage 1 forced, with their reasons

1. **Erbb4 is dropped** from the discriminating set. Its mouse transcript is 0.367
   log2 CPM when not targeted and exactly zero when targeted, so there is no receptor
   to remove and the contrast cannot discriminate anything.
2. **Egfr is flagged as low-abundance**, at 1.967 log2 CPM when not targeted, so its
   contrast is not read as equal to Erbb2 or Erbb3.
3. **A fibroblast depth floor of 100,000 counts** governs the reading of any single
   well, and two depth-restricted sensitivities are declared. At that depth a gene at
   50 counts per million is expected about five times, so a 54-gene mean is a
   programme estimate; below it the score reports detection. Ten axis or control wells
   fall below the floor and their single-well contrasts are reported as
   uninterpretable, among them Erbb2 in unit 3-1 at 1,759 counts and Itgb6 in 3-3 at
   4,865.
4. **The control wells lose their anchor role** and become descriptive context, because
   four of the eight plate-3 control wells are below the floor.
5. **The epithelial covariate is fixed as the count-based epithelial fraction**, mouse
   total over the sum of mouse and human totals. The contract said read fraction; the
   count-based version comes from the same counts the endpoint uses and is
   reproducible from a tracked table.

The primary reference set keeps every well. Excluding wells would change the rank
universe, so the depth restrictions are sensitivities rather than filters on the
primary. All four Areg wells clear the floor, which the freeze checks and refuses to
proceed without.

## What stage 3 may and may not do

It may compute the two endpoints on the plate-3 wells, residualize, rank, and report
the primary with its three sensitivities, the declared depth diagnostic, and the
discriminating contrasts without a decision attached. It may not redefine an
endpoint, change the direction, drop a well from the primary, promote a sensitivity
to the primary, or read an uninterpretable well as evidence. It must verify the
per-library totals against the A10 extraction record before scoring anything, and it
must refuse to overwrite an existing output.
